What the fee covers

Your fee isn't just paperwork — it pays for the checks and visits that make sure your building work is safe and signed off.

Checking your plans

A surveyor reviews your drawings against the building regulations before work starts.

Site inspections

Visits at key stages of the build to check the work is being done correctly.

Advice along the way

Support from your surveyor if questions come up during the work.

Your completion certificate

The final sign-off you'll need when selling or remortgaging your home.

Paying in stages. For some application types the fee is split — a plan charge when you apply, and an inspection charge later. We'll always show this clearly before you pay.
